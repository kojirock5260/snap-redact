Select an area, hide it, box it, point at it, then straight to the clipboard. Nothing leaves your machine.

Snap Redact is for the moment you want to paste part of a page into Slack, a bug report, or a doc, with something blacked out and something pointed at. One trip: select, mark, copy. No editor tab, no account, no upload.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Click the toolbar icon, use the right-click menu, or press Cmd+Shift+E (Ctrl+Shift+E on Windows and Linux).
2. Drag an area, or click an element to select exactly it. Cmd+A / Ctrl+A selects the whole visible page.
3. Hide (1), Box (2) or Arrow (3). Drag inside the selection to draw, or click an element to hide or box it precisely.
4. Copy (Cmd+C or Enter) or save as PNG (Cmd+S). Done.

REDACTION THAT CANNOT BE REVERSED
Hide paints a solid, opaque block. There is no blur and no mosaic, on purpose: both can be reversed, and a screenshot you have already shared cannot be taken back. What is hidden is gone from the image.

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR MACHINE
- No server, no cloud, no analytics, no crash reports, no external fonts
- No storage permission: the extension keeps nothing, not even settings
- The capture is processed inside the page and handed straight to your clipboard or your downloads folder

NO INSTALL WARNINGS
Only three permissions: activeTab, scripting and contextMenus. None of them shows a warning. clipboardWrite and downloads are deliberately not requested.

MADE FOR
- Developers and QA filing bug reports
- Support teams sharing a customer's screen with account details hidden
- Anyone pasting screenshots into chat and docs

DETAILS
- Element pick: hover shows a dashed outline, click takes that element's rectangle. The page itself is never modified
- Move the selection with the arrow keys (Shift for 10px), resize it with eight handles
- Retina-quality output: the image is exported at device pixel size
- Use the keyboard shortcut to capture hover menus and tooltips without moving the mouse
- English and Japanese, following your browser language
- Area capture only. Full-page capture is deliberately not included
